ABSTRACT

Customer  Relationship  Management  (CRM)  systems  have  become  essential  tools  for  organizations  seeking  to
improve customer satisfaction, increase sales efficiency, and maintain long-term relationships. Many small and
medium enterprises still rely on manual or semi-digital processes, resulting in scattered customer information,
inconsistent communication, and reduced organizational productivity. This research focuses on the design and
development  of  a  web-based  CRM  system  that  integrates  customer  data  management,  lead  tracking,
communication history, employee performance monitoring, and support ticket handling into a unified platform.

The proposed CRM system utilizes a structured software engineering approach to ensure reliability, scalability,
and  user-friendly  interaction.  Core  features  implemented  include  role-  based  authentication,  centralized
customer  database,  lead  status  tracking,  automated  follow-ups,  and  real-time  dashboard  analytics.  Through
systematic  testing  and  evaluation,  the  system  demonstrates  significant  improvements  in  customer  response
time,  lead  conversion  accuracy,  and  task  automation  compared  to  traditional  manual  methods.  The  results
prove that the CRM system can serve as an efficient, cost-effective solution for organizations aiming to enhance
operational efficiency and data-driven decision-making.

I.

INTRODUCTION

Customer Relationship Management (CRM) has emerged as a critical component of modern business strategy,
driven  by  the  need  to  understand,  manage,  and  enhance  customer  interactions  throughout  their  lifecycle.  In
increasingly competitive markets, organizations must prioritize customer satisfaction, loyalty, and retention in
order  to  maintain  sustainable  growth.  CRM  systems  provide  an  integrated  framework  for  storing  customer
information,  tracking  sales  opportunities,  managing  support  activities,  and  enabling  data-driven  decision-
making. By consolidating customer-related processes into a single platform, CRM solutions help organizations
strengthen relationships, reduce operational inefficiencies, and improve service quality.

Despite the growing importance of CRM, many small and medium-sized enterprises still rely on traditional tools
such  as  spreadsheets,  manual  documentation,  and  fragmented  software  for  managing  customer  data.  These
methods often lead to inconsistencies, data loss, delayed follow- ups, and reduced productivity. Moreover, the
absence of  a  centralized  platform  makes  it  difficult  for  managers  to  monitor  performance,  evaluate  customer
interactions,  and  understand  sales  progress.  This  gap  highlights  the  need  for  an  accessible  and  customizable
CRM system tailored to the specific requirements of small businesses.

This  research  presents  the  design  and  development  of  a  web-based  CRM  system  aimed  at  addressing  these
challenges.  The  proposed  solution  integrates  essential  functions—customer  management,  lead  tracking,
employee assignment, support ticket handling, and analytical reporting—into a unified application. The system
leverages  modern  web  technologies  to  provide  a  user-friendly  interface,  secure  authentication,  and  efficient

Problem Statement:

In  today’s  highly  competitive  business  landscape,  organizations  interact  with  customers  across  multiple
channels,  including  phone  calls,  emails,  social  media,  and  in-person  meetings.  As  customer  expectations
continue to rise, businesses often struggle to manage these interactions effectively. With increasing volumes of
customer  data,  diverse  communication  touchpoints,  and  complex  sales  cycles,  it  has  become  challenging  for
companies to maintain streamlined and consistent customer relationship management processes.

Traditional methods such as Excel sheets, paper records, or disconnected software  tools are  inefficient, time-
consuming,  and  prone  to  human  error.  Employees  must  manually  record  customer  information,  track  leads,
schedule follow-ups, and manage complaints, which often results in incomplete data, missed opportunities, and
delayed  responses.  These  outdated  approaches  lack  automation  and  do  not  provide  actionable  insights,
hindering  an  organization’s  Furthermore,  the  absence  of  centralized  data  storage,  role-based  access  control,
and  secure  communication  channels  poses  significant  operational  and  security  risks.  Customer  information
stored  in  scattered  files  or  unsecured  systems  increases  the  likelihood  of  data  breaches,  loss  of  confidential
information,  and  reduced  accountability.  Additionally,  without  cloud-based  accessibility,  teams  cannot
collaborate efficiently, limiting productivity and slowing down the decision-making process.

As  a  result,  there  is  a  growing  need  for  a  modern,  automated,  and  secure  CRM  solution  that  consolidates  all
customer-related operations into a single platform. Such a system must enable organizations to store customer
data,  manage  leads,  track  sales  pipelines,  handle  support  tickets,  and  generate  real-time  analytical  reports.  It
should support scalability, ensure data privacy, enhance communication efficiency, and provide intuitive tools
that empower teams to offer consistent and personalized customer experiences.

The proposed CRM system addresses these challenges by delivering a centralized, secure, and analytics-
driven  customer  relationship  management  platform  that  optimizes  customer  interactions,  improves
sales effectiveness, and enhances organizational productivity.

scoring, automated reminders, and integration with external communication tools (email, SMS, WhatsApp). The
system will be designed with user-friendly navigation, responsive layouts, and intuitive interaction elements to
ensure ease of use for administrators, sales teams, and support staff.

II.

LITERATURE REVIEW

Customer  Relationship  Management  (CRM)  has  become  a  fundamental  component  of  business  operations,
enabling  organizations  to  manage  customer  interactions,  track  sales  processes,  and  improve  service  delivery.
Early studies emphasize that traditional approaches such as spreadsheets, paper records, and isolated software
tools result in fragmented data, inefficient communication, and limited visibility into customer behavior (Buttle,
2009).  These  limitations  hinder  timely  decision-making  and  reduce  customer  satisfaction,  especially  in
environments with large and diverse customer bases.

Modern  research  highlights  the  importance  of  adopting  integrated,  automated  CRM  systems  that  centralize
customer  data  and  streamline  workflows.  Payne  and  Frow  (2005)  argue  that  CRM  systems  enhance
organizational performance by combining technology, processes, and customer- focused strategies. Web-based
CRM  platforms  further  support  remote  accessibility,  real-time  updates,  and  collaboration  across  multiple
departments. Studies also show that features such as lead management, ticket tracking, data visualization, and
role-based access significantly improve sales effectiveness and service quality (Nguyen et al., 2015).

The literature increasingly points toward the value of scalable, user-friendly CRM solutions built using modern
technologies such as React, Node.js, and SQL databases. These systems not only automate routine tasks but also
provide  actionable  insights,  enabling  organizations  to  deliver  personalized  experiences  and  build  long-term
customer relationships.

secure data processing.

Limitations of the Existing System

1.  Manual Data Handling:

Customer  details,  leads,  and  support  requests  are  often  recorded  manually  in  spreadsheets  or  notebooks,
leading to errors and incomplete information.

2.  Lack of Centralized Storage:

Customer  data  is  scattered  across  multiple  files  and  platforms,  making  it  difficult  to  access,  update,  or
maintain consistency.

3.  No Automation:

Follow-ups,  lead  updates,  and  complaint  tracking  must  be  done  manually,  resulting  in missed opportunities
and delayed responses.

4.  Poor Data Security:

Existing methods do not offer secure authentication or encrypted storage, increasing the risk of data loss and
unauthorized access.

5.  No Real-Time Insights:

Traditional  systems  do  not  provide  dashboards,  analytics,  or  reports,  making  it  hard  for  businesses  to  track
performance or identify customer trends.

6.  Limited Collaboration:

Employees  cannot  share  or  update  information  easily,  leading  to  communication  gaps  and  inefficiencies  in
workflow.

7.  Low Scalability:

Existing  tools  cannot  handle  growing  customer  databases  or  increasing  operational  complexity.

IV.  PROPOSED SYSTEM

The  proposed  CRM  system  provides  a  centralized  and  automated  platform  to  manage  customer  data,  track
leads, and handle support tickets efficiently. It replaces manual processes with a secure, user-friendly system
that  offers  real-time  updates,  role-based  access,  and  visual  dashboards.  The  system  improves  data  accuracy,
enhances  communication,  and  supports  better  decision-making  through  organized  records  and  analytics.
Overall, it helps businesses streamline operations, improve customer service, and boost sales performance.

Key Features and Workflow:

1.  Data Acquisition and Storage:

○  Customer details, leads, follow-ups, and support tickets are recorded through the web interface and securely
stored in the MySQL database.

○  Role-based  authentication  ensures  Admin,  Sales,  and  Support  Staff  can  only  access  data  relevant  to  their
responsibilities.

2.  Data Preprocessing:

○  The system  validates  and  structures all  entered  customer and  lead  data  to  remove  duplicates,  errors,  and
incomplete records.

○  Consistent formatting ensures accurate tracking of customer profiles, lead statuses, and ticket histories.

V.

FUTURE ENHANCEMENT

While the current CRM system effectively manages customers, leads, and support tickets, several enhancements
can be implemented to further improve intelligence, usability, and business value:

1.  AI-Based Lead Scoring and Recommendation

Integrate  machine  learning  models  to  automatically  score  leads  based  on  past  conversion  patterns  and
recommend the next best action (call, email, follow-up date, etc.) for each prospect.

2.  Omnichannel Integration

Connect the CRM with email, WhatsApp, SMS, and social media platforms so that all customer conversations are
captured in one place, enabling unified communication history and faster responses.

3.  Mobile Application Development

Develop  Android  and  iOS  apps  to  allow  sales  and  support  teams  to  access  customer  data,  update  leads,  and
manage tickets on the go with push notifications for reminders and escalations.

4.  Advanced Analytics and Predictive Insights

Add  modules  for  sales  forecasting,  churn  prediction,  and  customer  lifetime  value  estimation  using  predictive
analytics dashboards to support strategic decision-making.

5.  Customer Feedback & Sentiment Analysis

Integrate  NLP-based  sentiment  analysis  on  feedback  forms,  emails,  and  chat  logs  to  automatically  detect
satisfaction levels, recurring issues, and improvement areas.

6.  Cloud-Based Deployment and Scalability

Host the CRM on cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud) to support large customer bases, multi-branch
organizations, and high-concurrency usage with auto- scaling.

7.  Role-Based Customization & Workflow Automation

Enable  configurable  workflows,  custom  fields,  and  rule-based  automation  (auto-assign  leads,  auto-close  stale
tickets, custom approval flows) so organizations can tailor the CRM to their unique processes.
