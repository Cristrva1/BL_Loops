ALM Intelligence Updates VerdictSearch

VerdictSearch Modernizes Verdict Cases and Settlements Tool New York, NY – July 10, 2018 — ALM Intelligence has released an upgraded version of VerdictSearch, the leading case-research tool for attorneys and insurers. VerdictSearch provides case-winning information for personal-injury attorneys on the plaintiff and defense side. The VerdictSearch database has more than 200,000 intricately fielded reports, and its user interface allows unparalleled specificity in searches. “In the ultra-competitive world of personal-injury law, attorneys and claims adjusters need every edge they can find to achieve the best possible resolution of a case,” says Glenn Koch, Editor in Chief, VerdictSearch . “Our customers are able to locate cases that precisely match their own case, for the purpose of valuation. Users also benefit from tracking the testimonial history of expert witnesses, how often they’ve testified, the attorneys and defendants who typically retain them, and what they’ve said in past cases.” The newly enhanced VerdictSearch tool includes:

  • Greater utility in combining case types and/or injuries
  • A telephone-research option for concierge service
  • Case-specific links to ALM Experts, for a deeper dive on expert-witness research
  • An improved user experience, greater speed and a more attractive visual display “We saw value in making our VerdictSearch tool even more case-specific and user-friendly, to provide the best possible research outcome,” says Andrew Neblett, President, ALM Intelligence . “ ALM Intelligence is dedicated to producing industry-leading products that help attorneys, law firms and other professionals achieve solutions across a broad spectrum of business needs.” To find out more about VerdictSearch and the tool’s upgrade, visit:http://verdictsearch.com/ About ALM Intelligence ALM Intelligence, a division of ALM Media LLC, supports legal, consulting, and benefits decision-makers seeking guidance on critical business challenges.
