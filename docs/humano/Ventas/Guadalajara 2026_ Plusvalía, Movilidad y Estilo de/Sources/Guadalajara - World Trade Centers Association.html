About World Trade Center Guadalajara

THE GATEWAY TO FOREIGN TRADE World Trade Center Guadalajara is the most important logistics hub for the movement of goods in Latin America. We provide logistics with the best connectivity for the transfer of goods from Mexico to other countries and back, with certified processes that guarantee the condition of your cargo. Tradition, innovation, freshness and e-commerce pass through our doors, connecting us to 192 countries due to our experience of over 27 years in handling and storing international trade management. Facilities & Services

We are the ideal partner for customs agents and freight forwarders, with the best security, connectivity, and infrastructure for foreign trade. OUR BUSINESS DIVISIONS AGWTC: A bonded warehouse focused on export, specializing in the handling of perishable products. CLA GDL: The most secure bonded warehouse focused on import specializing in pharmaceuticals and e-commerce. CLA PUEBLA: The leading logistics hub for the international transport of the key industries of southeast Mexico. GLOBAL OPERATIONS & INFRASTRUCTURE GROUP: We represent airlines with security and confidence.
