## Descripción Editorial
Larry Kendall's bestselling book Ninja Selling describes a system for selling real estate that focuses on building connections with clients and providing a positive, low-pressure sales experience. The majority of real estate professionals and salespeople who have used the book's ideas have given it excellent evaluations.

Among the fundamentals of Ninja Selling are:

acquiring an abundant mindset, emphasising providing value to customers and establishing lasting relationships rather than focused only on making quick sales.

using a "flow" approach to direct the sales process, which entails hearing clients out, finding out what their needs are, and then offering solutions in response.

developing a referral-based business by offering outstanding service and keeping committed customer connections.

Readers have praised Ninja a lot.
